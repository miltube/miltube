How to choose a paper core supplier

A core is a small component, but the wrong supplier choice affects your entire winding line. Here are the questions to ask before committing.

1. Can they give you test results, not just a data sheet?

A generic data sheet shows theoretical ranges. What actually matters is the measured result for the batch you will actually receive: radial and axial compression strength, moisture content, straightness. A serious supplier tests every batch in-house and can share those results with you.

2. Do they truly manufacture to order?

Many suppliers only offer a limited number of fixed sizes. If your application is specific — a non-standard diameter, an unusual length, a tight tolerance — check that the supplier can adjust inside diameter, wall thickness and cut length to your exact specifications, not just pick from a fixed catalog.

3. Will they send you a sample before you order?

Testing a core on your actual line, before committing to volume, reveals problems no data sheet shows: fit on the shaft, behavior under tension, stability in storage. A supplier confident in its quality is happy to send a sample.

4. Can they guarantee reliable lead times?

Running out of cores stops an entire production line. Ask for clear lead times, and check the supplier's ability to hold a recurring delivery schedule, not just fill a one-off first order.

5. Can they provide export-ready documentation if needed?

If you're importing, check that the supplier can provide technical data sheets, certificates of conformity and packing lists for customs clearance, without extra delay once the order is ready.

In summary

A good core supplier isn't judged on price alone, but on their ability to prove, with real numbers, that every batch delivered will hold up on your line.
