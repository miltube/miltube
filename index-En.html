Precision spiral winding technology

Our cores are manufactured by spiral-winding multiple layers of premium kraft and testliner board, bonded with high-grade silicate or vinyl adhesives. Each tube is clean-cut to custom length and dimensionally stabilized.

  • 4,000mm max cut length
  • ±1.5mm length tolerance
  • 3–17mm wall thickness range
  • 100%recyclable material

In-house laboratory, verified performance

Every production batch goes through our quality-control laboratory before it leaves the plant. We test mechanical strength, dimensional accuracy and moisture content, so your winding lines run without failures.

  • Radial crush resistance

    Measures the core's resistance to the gripping forces applied during high-tension winding. Vital for high-speed converters.

  • Axial compression test

    Validates load capacity for vertical storage and stacking. Prevents deformation during warehousing.

  • Dimensional stability

    After thermal conditioning, cores are measured for straightness (max 1.5 mm/m) to counter ambient humidity shifts.

  • Moisture management

    Kept between 4 and 6% for structural integrity. Checked on every batch with calibrated digital hygrometers.
